# SigmaTactix Growth OS — Domain Glossary

Ubiquitous domain language for SigmaTactix Growth OS, the AI-native growth infrastructure and multi-tenant marketing operating system.

## Workspace & Tenancy

**Client Enclave**:
A cryptographically and logically isolated workspace tenant housing all analytics, vaults, and agent runs for an individual organization or portfolio brand.
_Avoid_: Account, company profile, organization folder, tenant instance, white-label subaccount

**Sovereign Growth Infrastructure**:
A fully owned, in-house marketing intelligence and autonomous swarm system operating under the exclusive control of an internal growth team or fractional growth executive.
_Avoid_: White-label platform, reseller software, outsourced agency portal

**In-House Mode**:
The single-enclave operational view emphasizing customer acquisition cost (CAC) reduction, pipeline velocity, and full-time employee (FTE) / agency replacement savings.
_Avoid_: Single-tenant mode, standard dashboard

**Portfolio Mode**:
The multi-enclave operational view for Fractional CMOs and growth partners, comparing growth velocity, burn rate, and AI swarm health across multiple client enclaves.
_Avoid_: Agency view, reseller admin, multi-account dashboard

**Intake Token**:
A single-use, time-expiring cryptographic token granting a prospective client or brand access to the onboarding audit wizard.
_Avoid_: Invite link, signup code, onboarding key

**Playbook**:
The canonical four-phase growth operating loop: Diagnostics & Audit, Data & Vault Ingestion, Swarm Automation, and Revenue Core.
_Avoid_: Onboarding sequence, growth funnel, setup wizard

## Second Brain & Knowledge

**Knowledge Vault**:
One of ten specialized, schema-enforced knowledge repositories partitioned by business domain.
_Avoid_: Folder, bucket, directory, database table

**Secret Vault**:
An ultra-restricted knowledge vault (specifically Executive Vault and Credentials Vault) inaccessible to autonomous agents under all circumstances.
_Avoid_: Private folder, locked vault, admin bucket

**Topical Authority**:
The semantic density and entity relationships established across a domain to command organic visibility and AI citations.
_Avoid_: Keyword ranking, SEO score, backlink strength

**Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)**:
The structured engineering of brand content and entity markup to guarantee prominent citation by generative AI answer engines.
_Avoid_: AI SEO, LLM search optimization, bot scraping

## Autonomous Swarms & Intelligence

**Swarm**:
A coordinated cluster of specialized autonomous AI agents executing synchronized marketing, analysis, and outreach tasks under strict RBAC constraints.
_Avoid_: Bot army, worker pool, automated script

**Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)**:
The state machine requiring affirmative human authorization before an agent can perform external, destructive, or budget-altering actions.
_Avoid_: Manual check, approval gate, admin review

**Threshold-Based Autonomy**:
An execution policy where AI swarms automatically scrape, enrich, and stage intelligence above a 90% confidence score, while reserving external message dispatch strictly for human authorization.
_Avoid_: Unattended automation, auto-bot posting, manual-only mode

**High-Signal Action Queue**:
A dedicated, high-prominence staging deck on the Executive Dashboard showcasing high-intent competitor vulnerabilities, lead signals, and drafted battlecards awaiting 1-click human execution.
_Avoid_: Notification center, pending alerts, task list

**VANTA**:
The Sigma Intelligence multimodal neural core powering real-time system state perception, voice interaction, and living topology inspection.
_Avoid_: Voice bot, AI assistant, floating widget

**Agent Reach**:
The multi-platform intelligence router extracting real-time public data across 15 external developer, social, video, and web networks.
_Avoid_: Web scraper, social bot, internet search tool

**SDR Battlecard**:
A concise, structured sales intelligence briefing detailing a specific competitor's verified vulnerabilities, pricing traps, and counter-positioning scripts.
_Avoid_: Pitch deck, competitor sheet, sales cheat sheet

**Growth Matrix**:
An empirical eight-category diagnostic evaluation assessing an organization's digital growth readiness, conversion health, and operational maturity.
_Avoid_: Scorecard, performance review, audit report
