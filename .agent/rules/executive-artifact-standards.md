# Executive Artifact & Anti-Slop Standards

When generating client proposals, CRO audits, landing page drafts, or executive deliverables:

## 1. Zero AI Slop & Human-Sounding Voice

- **Banned Words & Buzzwords**: Never use `delve`, `foster`, `leverage`, `utilize`, `streamline`, `robust`, `cutting-edge`, `game-changer`, `paradigm shift`, `multifaceted`, `intricate`, `transformative`, `elevate`, `supercharge`, `tapestry`.
- **No Faux Insight or Throat-Clearing**: Ban phrases like `"Here's the thing"`, `"What most people miss"`, `"The reality is"`, `"It's important to note"`.
- **No Binary Contrasts or Dramatic Colons**: Ban `"This is not X. It's Y."` and fake drama colons (`"The key: it works"`). State the fact directly.
- **Portability Test**: If a sentence or paragraph could move unchanged to another company or vertical, it is filler—replace it with concrete operational facts, role titles, and specific GCC mechanisms.
- **Strict Evidence Guard**: Never invent conversion promises, guarantees, or synthetic percentages (`+X%`, `guaranteed`).

## 2. Distinctive Visual Architecture

- **No Identical Boilerplate**: Never reuse generic 3-step cards across different companies. Every prospect must have copy, KPIs, workflows, and terminology tailored directly to their specific operational reality.
- **Product UI Mockups**: Include realistic, high-fidelity browser or terminal window chrome (`● ● ●`, URL bar, live status badge) displaying authentic tables, telemetry metrics, or dispatch feeds.
- **Before vs. After Operational Matrices**: Explicitly contrast the chaotic status quo (manual paper, WhatsApp chats, delayed reconciliations) against governed OS automation.
- **Bespoke Visual Process Flow**: Provide custom SVG-powered pipeline diagrams detailing the technical and compliance milestones (e.g. Ingestion → Contract Validation → SIF Remittance).

## 3. Typography & Spacing Hygiene

- **No Squished Letters**: Headings must never use negative letter-spacing that collides characters (keep between `-0.015em` and `0`).
- **Comfortable Line Heights**: Body copy `1.65` to `1.7`; headings `1.15` to `1.25`.
- **Generous Whitespace**: Section padding minimum `80px 0` on desktop, card padding `32px`, minimum gap `24px`.
- **Minimum Font Floor**: Respect accessible font sizes (`12px` / `0.75rem` minimum floor, never sub-11px micro-text).
