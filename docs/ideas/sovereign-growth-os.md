# Sovereign Growth OS — Product Concept One-Pager

## Problem Statement
How Might We equip B2B SaaS growth teams and Fractional CMOs with a sovereign, AI-orchestrated growth infrastructure that autonomously discovers competitor vulnerabilities, extracts real-time market signals across 15 channels, and drafts high-authority content and SDR battlecards—without the drag of $15k/mo outsourced agency retainers or the risk of white-label commoditization?

## Recommended Direction
SigmaTactix Growth OS operates as an exclusive, client-owned intelligence command deck rather than a white-labeled agency commodity. It delivers a **Hybrid In-House & Portfolio Architecture**:
1. **In-House Mode**: Provides single-organization leadership with empirical proof of CAC reduction, pipeline acceleration, and agency headcount replacement ROI.
2. **Portfolio Mode**: Allows Fractional CMOs and elite growth advisors to command 5–10 client enclaves from a unified cockpit with cross-enclave health benchmarks.

External intelligence is gathered via **Agent Reach** across 15 developer, social, video, and web networks under **Threshold-Based Autonomy**: high-confidence (>90%) signals auto-generate SDR battlecards and counter-positioning drafts into a prominent **High-Signal Action Queue** on the Executive Dashboard, reserving final external dispatch strictly for 1-click human authorization.

The system's living state is inspected and queried on-demand through a persistent **VANTA Header Node** that triggers a slide-out neural transcript drawer, while maintaining a dedicated 3D neural core at `/living-system`.

## Key Assumptions to Validate
- [ ] **Assumption 1**: In-house B2B founders and CMOs find "Agency Spend Replacement ROI" ($18k/mo saved vs $400 cloud run cost) significantly more compelling than traditional software pricing tiers. *Validation: Test messaging in Executive Dashboard Topline KPI row.*
- [ ] **Assumption 2**: A slide-out neural drawer triggered from the top navbar is less disruptive and more frequently utilized than a fixed bottom floating dock across 28 pages. *Validation: Measure drawer open/close frequency and session length.*
- [ ] **Assumption 3**: Staging AI-discovered leads in a dedicated "High-Signal Action Queue" on the main dashboard increases SDR outreach execution rate by >3x compared to buried sub-page tabs. *Validation: Track 1-click dispatch latency from dashboard.*

## MVP Scope
- **In Scope**:
  - Topline Executive Dashboard equipped with the **High-Signal Action Queue** highlight deck.
  - Hybrid ROI view toggling between In-House CAC/Headcount savings and Portfolio Enclave health.
  - Top header **VANTA Neural Status Widget** opening the interactive slide-out transcript drawer with full workspace context.
  - Backend modular route decomposition into 5 domain routers (`src/server/routes/*`) ensuring clean code seams.
- **Out of Scope (For Now)**:
  - Full bidirectional WebSockets audio streaming (deferred to Phase 4; text and workspace state Q&A enabled first).
  - Multi-tenant client billing/invoicing automation (de-prioritized since platform is sovereign, not reseller-facing).

## Not Doing (and Why)
- **Not Doing White-Labeling**: The owner maintains sovereign control of the brand and IP. White-labeling commoditizes advanced agent infrastructure and invites support drag from low-tier agencies.
- **Not Doing Unattended Autonomous Social Posting**: Auto-posting without human review risks brand reputation and account bans. Threshold-based autonomy drafts content, but dispatch requires affirmative HITL authorization.
- **Not Doing Persistent Obstructive Bottom Dock**: A permanent floating bar across all 28 pages clutters data-dense analytics tables; replaced by an elegant, accessible header neural status node.
