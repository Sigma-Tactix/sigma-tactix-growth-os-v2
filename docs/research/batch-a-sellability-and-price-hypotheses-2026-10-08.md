# Batch A — sellability and price hypotheses

**Prepared:** 8 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Decide which narrowly scoped, founder-led service conversations are worth testing first. This is **not** evidence that any company is seeking services, has budget, or will accept the prices below. It contains no outreach, contact enrichment, or customer-data analysis.

## Reading this correctly

- An **observable signal** is a public website pattern such as a trial, a demo path, an active vertical-content programme, or a broad product story. It is not buyer intent.
- Prices are Sigma Tactix delivery hypotheses, in AED, for manual/supervised work. They are not quotes, market rates, or evidence of price willingness.
- The existing AED 27,500 governed competitor-to-conversion sprint remains the approved full-sprint offer. The smaller options below are entry services only; they do not replace it.
- Every deliverable remains subject to founder review. No paid-media changes, publication, CRM writes, automations, or client-system access are included.

## 1. Blueberry ERP — medium confidence

### Observable public signal

Blueberry has a consultation and price-estimation path, a stated discovery-to-go-live implementation journey, and current contractor/MEP articles including material-request, project-estimate, and journal-review topics. This is public evidence of an active vertical sales story and consultation-led funnel—not evidence that conversion is underperforming. [Blueberry homepage](https://blueberry.software/)

### Credible service to offer now

**Contractor conversion-evidence diagnostic.** Review the public MEP/contractor journey, map one visible “estimate to cost-control” story, and supply a wireframe plus approved copy outline for one landing page and a post-consultation implementation explainer.

**One-time diagnostic hypothesis:** AED 4,500.  
**30-day supervised pilot hypothesis:** AED 15,000.

The pilot would produce the approved page/copy assets, desktop/mobile renderings, a comparison rationale, and a handover pack. It excludes access to ERP instances, project/customer records, migration data, security assessment, or claims validation.

## 2. Fleetroot — medium confidence

### Observable public signal

Fleetroot has two distinct acquisition motions: a no-card, 14-day trial and a tailored demo that asks about fleet size and cargo. Its pricing separates small-team, growing-operation, and enterprise paths; enterprise also points to onboarding and integrations. That breadth supports a hypothesis that industry-specific proof and first-value content may be useful, but it does not establish a trial-conversion problem. [Pricing](https://fleetroot.com/pricing/) · [Demo page](https://fleetroot.com/fleet-management-software-demo-fleetroot/)

### Credible service to offer now

**Trial-and-demo journey diagnostic.** Review the public trial, demo, plan, and selected industry pages; recommend one vertical path and a first-value sequence built around a publicly stated workflow such as dispatch, tracking, or proof of delivery.

**One-time diagnostic hypothesis:** AED 5,500.  
**30-day supervised pilot hypothesis:** AED 18,000.

The pilot would deliver one vertical landing-page prototype, approved supporting copy, a trial/onboarding content sequence, and an evidence pack. It excludes customer operational data, telematics, rider/driver data, production API work, integrations, automated email/SMS sending, and performance guarantees.

## 3. jalebi.io — low confidence

### Observable public signal

jalebi offers a free demo and an early-adopter beta price, explicitly invites customers to co-create the product, and publicly frames a broad product story across inventory, POS, menu, reporting, and operations. Its English pages also contain market-presence inconsistency noted in the Batch A brief, so a message and offer-consistency review is more defensible than asserting a conversion defect. [Pricing](https://jalebi.io/price/) · [About](https://jalebi.io/get-to-know-us/)

### Credible service to offer now

**Restaurant buyer-message and offer-path diagnostic.** Review the public English/Arabic buyer journey, distinguish substantiated product claims from claims needing confirmation, and create a single restaurant-operator comparison/demo-page outline.

**One-time diagnostic hypothesis:** AED 5,500.  
**30-day supervised pilot hypothesis:** AED 16,500.

The pilot would supply a claim-controlled demo-page prototype, one product-story content asset, and a handover/approval register. It excludes POS, order, inventory, customer, supplier, payment, or restaurant data; pricing experiments; publishing; translations represented as legal/commercially approved; and outcome claims.

## 4. HRDesk — medium confidence

### Observable public signal

HRDesk has a seven-day self-serve trial, simple per-seat pricing, a large all-in-one feature catalogue, and a public “go live” claim. This gives a clear public activation journey to examine. It does **not** prove the trial is failing, and the service must stay away from payroll, employee, visa, attendance, or bank data. [HRDesk pricing](https://hrdesk.ae/pricing) · [Homepage](https://hrdesk.ae/)

### Credible service to offer now

**Role-based trial activation diagnostic.** Evaluate the public HR, finance, and operations messaging; choose one first-value path (for example, a non-data-bearing onboarding walkthrough); and propose a claims-reviewed landing and onboarding-content sequence.

**One-time diagnostic hypothesis:** AED 5,500.  
**30-day supervised pilot hypothesis:** AED 17,500.

The pilot would deliver role-specific page/copy prototypes, an approved non-production trial guide, and a measurement plan using aggregate metrics only if HRDesk later supplies them. It excludes HR/payroll records, compliance/legal review, security testing, bank/WPS file handling, system integrations, automated lifecycle campaigns, and publishing.

## 5. PropSpace — medium confidence

### Observable public signal

PropSpace offers a one-week demo and personalised consultation while maintaining active, dated content for brokerage and property-management workflows, lead capture, API availability, and UAE real-estate topics. This creates a public-content-to-demo path worth reviewing, but it is not proof that its marketing is ineffective or that it has a service budget. [About](https://www.propspace.com/about) · [Insights](https://www.propspace.com/blog)

### Credible service to offer now

**Brokerage-versus-property-management conversion diagnostic.** Map the public paths for the two audiences, identify overlapping or missing proof points, and produce an annotated wireframe for one audience-specific page plus an associated content-to-demo path.

**One-time diagnostic hypothesis:** AED 6,500.  
**30-day supervised pilot hypothesis:** AED 20,000.

The pilot would deliver one approved page prototype, content conversion map, comparison annotations, and a measurement specification. It excludes CRM/lead/tenant/property data, portal/API access, RERA or legal review, production integrations, automated lead handling, and publication.

## Ranking for a first service conversation

1. **Blueberry ERP** — clearest narrowly defined contractor story; lowest-scope public-evidence diagnostic.
2. **Fleetroot** — visible trial/demo segmentation supports a focused acquisition-path review.
3. **PropSpace** — sustained current content offers multiple evidence points, with a more complex product/market scope.
4. **HRDesk** — strong public activation motion, but sensitivity boundaries materially narrow the available work.
5. **jalebi.io** — potentially useful positioning work, but public market/offer inconsistency raises validation risk.

## Capacity and revenue model — not a forecast

| --- | --- | ---: |
| Diagnostics only | Five founder-led public-evidence diagnostics | AED 27,500 |
| One pilot after one diagnostic | One diagnostic plus one 30-day pilot | AED 19,500–26,500 |
| All five diagnostic-to-pilot sequences | Five diagnostics plus five 30-day pilots | AED 114,500 |

The full five-pilot scenario would require a small delivery pod or staged starts; it is not suitable for a single person to run concurrently while rebuilding Growth OS. A conservative capacity plan is **one 30-day pilot at a time** until delivery templates, approvals, and artifact QA have been tested.

## Decision rule

No company should be told that it “needs” a service. The correct founder-led opening is an offer to validate a specific public-evidence hypothesis in a paid diagnostic. There is **no public evidence here of willingness to pay**, procurement authority, current budget, or buying timeline. A price is only validated after a real conversation, scope confirmation, and written approval.
