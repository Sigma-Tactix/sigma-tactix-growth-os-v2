# Batch A — public-evidence brief

**Purpose:** Inputs for five internal, personalised opportunity briefs. This is not contact enrichment, customer intelligence, proof that any company is a buyer, or authority to send outreach.

**Research boundary:** Company-owned public pages only. Sources were accessed on **2026-10-08**. Product, customer-count, security, outcome, market-presence, and leadership statements below are attributed to the respective company and are **not independently audited by Sigma Tactix**. Each conversion opportunity is a hypothesis to validate, not a diagnosis.

## 1. Blueberry ERP

### Validated public facts

- Blueberry presents its product as a UAE contracting ERP with integrated sales, accounting, inventory, HR, operations, and project/task capabilities; its project flow mentions estimates, contracts/subcontracts, cost/income tracking, and resources. [Homepage](https://blueberry.software/)
- Its stated delivery journey is discovery, data understanding/migration, customisation/development, and go-live. [Homepage](https://blueberry.software/)
- The price-estimation form asks about industry, requested modules, user count, and email; the primary commercial path is a consultation. [Price estimation](https://blueberry.software/PriceEstimation/)
- Its public policy says the app can collect facial data for identity, attendance, and facial-recognition features with consent. [Privacy policy](https://blueberry.software/privacy-policy/)

### Hypotheses to validate

1. A visual MEP/contractor evidence page could turn existing cost-control topics into a clear “material request → estimate → journal review” story and make consultation value easier to understand.
2. A short post-form implementation explainer could reduce uncertainty about migration, customisation, and go-live for qualified prospects.

### Limitations and claims to avoid

- Do not state pricing, named customer outcomes, certified security posture, a live demo, or a confirmed buying owner: none was established from the reviewed official pages.
- Do not turn its marketing language into a comparative “best” or “most flexible” claim.
- The policy contains a placeholder contact-email field; treat this as a content-quality observation only, not a privacy or security finding.

**Recommended one-page brief focus:** “Make the contracting cost-control journey visible before the consultation.”

## 2. Fleetroot

### Validated public facts

- Fleetroot presents telematics and transport-operations capabilities across vehicles, drivers, devices, orders, routes, and deliveries; its site describes real-time visibility, routing, dispatch, and proof of delivery. [Homepage](https://fleetroot.com/)
- The site has industry framing for healthcare/pharma, e-commerce/last-mile, commercial fleets, and distribution/3PL. [Homepage](https://fleetroot.com/)
- Its public pricing offers a 14-day, no-card trial; listed plans scale by tasks, with Enterprise described as custom for higher volume, SLAs, and integrations. [Pricing](https://fleetroot.com/pricing/)
- Its demo form asks for fleet size and what the prospect moves, and promises a tailored walkthrough. [Request a demo](https://fleetroot.com/fleet-management-software-demo-fleetroot/)

### Hypotheses to validate

1. Industry-specific landing paths could pair the existing problem-to-capability content with attributed customer stories and a cargo/fleet-size-specific demo CTA; test them against the general trial and demo paths.
2. A post-trial activation sequence centred on one first-use outcome—first route, tracked vehicle, or proof of delivery—could help prospects reach initial value sooner.

### Limitations and claims to avoid

- Do not claim a conversion baseline, trial-to-paid issue, buying owner, or independently verified customer outcome.
- Case-study outcomes and claims such as GDPR compliance, OWASP alignment, and annual pen-testing are company claims, not Sigma verification. [Homepage](https://fleetroot.com/)

**Recommended one-page brief focus:** “Turn a broad fleet platform into an industry-specific path from operational pain to first measurable workflow.”

## 3. jalebi.io

### Validated public facts

- jalebi calls itself an inventory-first restaurant operating system and lists inventory, CRM, operations, recipes, menus, orders, analytics, and reporting as part of its product scope. [Homepage](https://jalebi.io/en/)
- Its about page identifies the company as a B2B F&B platform focused on operational problems such as food cost, wastage, theft, resupply, and oversight; it identifies a CEO and CPO. [About](https://jalebi.io/get-to-know-us/)
- Its homepage presents a restaurant-operator discovery story and an explicit product framing around food-cost management. [Homepage](https://jalebi.io/en/)
- Market-presence messaging is inconsistent across official pages: its about-page banner lists KSA, UAE, and Oman as live, while the homepage FAQ says it is launching for partners in KSA. [About](https://jalebi.io/get-to-know-us/) · [Homepage FAQ](https://jalebi.io/en/)

### Hypotheses to validate

1. A comparison-led demo page could explain the difference between connected restaurant operations and disconnected point tools, using only claims jalebi can substantiate.
2. A short “from purchase to food cost” product narrative could make the inventory-first positioning easier for restaurant operators to evaluate before they request a trial or conversation.

### Limitations and claims to avoid

- Do not state the company’s active markets, pricing, customer results, or product uniqueness without confirmation from jalebi.
- Do not claim that jalebi has a conversion problem or use the site’s food-cost/efficiency promises as guaranteed outcomes.

**Recommended one-page brief focus:** “Clarify the inventory-first restaurant operating story and its proof path for a restaurant decision-maker.”

## 4. HRDesk

### Validated public facts

- HRDesk describes a UAE-focused HR platform with 15 modules and 150+ features, covering payroll, attendance, WPS/gratuity, leave, recruitment, performance, reporting, and related functions. [Homepage](https://hrdesk.ae/)
- The site’s main conversion paths are a seven-day free trial and product demonstration; it states a starting price of AED 9.99 per employee per month. [Homepage](https://hrdesk.ae/) · [Pricing](https://hrdesk.ae/pricing)
- The public site uses UAE-specific operating themes—WPS/SIF files, gratuity, visa-document alerts, Emiratisation tracking, and bank formats—as its category framing. [Homepage](https://hrdesk.ae/)
- Its about page presents a “replace spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and paper registers” mission and says it supports Arabic- and English-speaking humans. [About](https://hrdesk.ae/about)

### Hypotheses to validate

1. Sector-specific proof pages could convert the existing broad feature set into role-based stories for HR, finance, and operations decision-makers.
2. A short trial-to-first-payroll or trial-to-first-compliance-workflow sequence could make the product’s “go live” promise observable rather than merely promotional.

### Limitations and claims to avoid

- Public customer counts conflict: the homepage says 267+ companies, while the about page says 150+; do not reuse either total until HRDesk confirms it.
- Do not independently assert compliance, encryption, uptime, legal accuracy, or time-to-go-live. These are supplier claims and may need legal/product validation.
- Do not use employee, payroll, visa, or HR records in any sample work.

**Recommended one-page brief focus:** “Convert UAE-HR compliance messages into a role-specific proof journey from first trial action to verified value.”

## 5. PropSpace

### Validated public facts

- PropSpace describes its real-estate CRM as serving brokers, property managers, administrators, and marketers, with centralised workflows, automation, and data-driven/collaborative capabilities. [About](https://www.propspace.com/about)
- Its official history says the company was founded in 2012, was acquired by Property Finder in 2019, and became independent as PropTech Systems Ltd in the DIFC after a 2024 acquisition. [About](https://www.propspace.com/about)
- Its blog has current 2026 content across brokerage CRM, property management, public APIs, lead automation, property portals, and UAE regulatory topics. [Blog](https://www.propspace.com/blog)
- The site offers a one-week demo and personalised consultation. [About](https://www.propspace.com/about)

### Hypotheses to validate

1. A decision path that separates brokerage and property-management buyers could help visitors reach relevant proof, workflows, and demonstration material faster.
2. A conversion asset that maps current integrations, public APIs, and compliance-oriented content to specific buyer jobs could reduce the effort needed to understand the breadth of the platform.

### Limitations and claims to avoid

- Do not state independent leadership, customer, security, compliance, or growth verification; the reviewed facts are supplier-published.
- Do not use real-estate customer, tenant, lead, or transaction data in a sample.
- Do not represent historic blog outcomes or company positioning as a present buying need.

**Recommended one-page brief focus:** “Make the right product proof discoverable for brokerage versus property-management buyer journeys.”

## Use protocol

1. Founder selects one company and confirms the allowed scope before a bespoke brief is designed.
2. The brief cites only the relevant facts above, separates observation from hypothesis, and names what needs the company’s confirmation.
3. No claim, asset, outreach copy, or proposal is published or sent without founder review and approval.
